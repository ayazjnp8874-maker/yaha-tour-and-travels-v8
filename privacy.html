Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

YAHA TOUR AND TRAVELS ("we", "our") uses the information you submit to handle travel enquiries and bookings.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect your name, mobile number, email address, pickup and destination details, travel date and time, vehicle choice, trip distance, booking status and related messages.

How we use information

We use booking details to respond to enquiries, arrange vehicles or drivers, manage bookings, provide customer support and maintain booking records.

Services used

The app may use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to sign users in and store booking and account information. When you choose WhatsApp booking, your device opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. WhatsApp is operated by Meta and its own privacy terms apply.

Payments

This app does not currently collect online payments. Any fare or payment arrangement is confirmed separately with the customer.

Sharing and retention

Booking information may be visible to authorized administrators and the driver assigned to a booking when needed to provide the service. We retain information for operational and record-keeping purposes and do not promise a fixed deletion period in this policy.

Your choices

You may contact us to request correction or deletion of information, subject to records we need to retain for legitimate business or legal purposes.
